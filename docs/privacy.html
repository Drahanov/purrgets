Privacy Policy

Purrgets for iPhone and Mac · Effective October 3, 2026

Purrgets is a countdown app with home screen widgets. It is built to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Purrgets has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party services. The app does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

The countdowns you create and your app settings are saved only on your device. They are shared between the Purrgets app and its widgets so the widgets can show your countdowns. This data never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Calendar access

Purrgets can ask for access to your calendar so you can turn an event into a countdown. This is optional. Calendar events are read on your device only to fill in a countdown, and they are never uploaded or shared. You can turn access off at any time in your device's Settings.

Children

Purrgets does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
